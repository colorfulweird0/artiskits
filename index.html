Where your money goes

Every box is a direct
investment in local art.

We believe artists should be paid fairly for their creativity. When you subscribe, you’re not buying from a warehouse — you’re commissioning a local creative. Proceeds from every ArtisKit are split evenly: 50% goes directly to the artist who made it, and 50% goes to Safe Work Coalition, a nonprofit that builds community for sex workers and connects them to trusted resources.

  • 50% to the artist — featured artists earn from every kit they create.
  • 50% to Safe Work Coalition — building community and access to housing, healthcare and legal resources.
  • Families creating — screen-free time that brings people together.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Who are ArtisKits made for?

Everyone! Our kits are designed to be enjoyed by families, kids with a grown-up helper, and adults looking for a creative break. Each guide notes the recommended age and skill level.

Who puts the kits together?

Each month a featured local artist designs the theme, chooses the materials and hand-assembles the kits. You’ll find their story and a personal note inside every box.

Where do the proceeds go?

Proceeds are split 50/50. Half goes directly to the artist who created that month’s kit, and half goes to Safe Work Coalition, a nonprofit that supports sex workers by building community and connecting them to trusted resources.

When will my kit ship?

Kits ship on the 7th of each month. Subscribe by the 4th to receive that month’s kit.

Can I skip a month or cancel?

Yes. You can skip, pause or cancel anytime from your account — no fees, no hassle.

I’m a local artist. How can I create a kit?

We’d love to hear from you! Fill out our artist application with a bit about your work and a kit idea, and we’ll be in touch.
